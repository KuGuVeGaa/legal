We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, or phone number directly, unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., via support emails). However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to improve our App's stability and user experience. These services may collect:
- Device information (model, OS version, unique device identifiers).
- Usage data (sessions, features used, crash logs).
- Location data (approximate location derived from IP address).

### C. Advertising Data
We use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and use:
- Advertising ID (GAID/IDFA).
- IP address.
- Usage data to show personalized or non-personalized ads.
